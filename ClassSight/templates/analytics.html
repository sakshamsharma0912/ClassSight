Analysis rules

Marks, attendance and risk calculations

Grade guide
Academic pass

At least 40/100 in every selected course, with every component evaluated. Overall averages use equal course weighting.

At Risk & Watchlist

At Risk: a completed course below 40, evaluated average below 50, or course attendance below 75%. Watchlist: evaluated average below 65 or eligible attendance below 85%.

Pending results

Missing components never count as zero in saved results or charts. Overall grades and ranks stay pending until every selected course evaluation is complete.
